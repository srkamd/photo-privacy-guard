Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 28, 2026 • Last Updated: October 28, 2026

1. Overview & Privacy Philosophy

Photo Privacy Guard ("we", "our", or "the Service") is built from the ground up as a zero-knowledge, privacy-first image and document sanitization utility. We believe your private photos, GPS locations, and identity documents belong solely to you.

2. Zero Data Retention Policy

When you upload or submit an image for metadata inspection, Ghost Mode location injection, or watermark protection, the image is processed purely in ephemeral server memory (RAM) and immediately returned to you. We do not store, archive, log, sell, or train AI models on your photos.

3. Data Collected & Processed

The Service only processes the specific image binary data you explicitly supply during a session. No personal identifying information (PII), email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials are collected or tracked.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not share, transmit, or disclose any user images or extracted metadata to third parties, advertising networks, or analytics trackers.

5. Security & Encryption

All data transmitted between your device, ChatGPT, and our serverless infrastructure is protected using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS 1.3) encryption.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or inquiries regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact developer Shahrukh Ahmad at srkamd.dev@gmail.com.
