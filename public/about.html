Safeguarding Visual Privacy
In The Artificial Intelligence Era

Photo Privacy Guard is an open, zero-retention metadata sanitization suite designed for both modern web apps and the ChatGPT Model Context Protocol (MCP) ecosystem.

⚠️ The Hidden Danger in Everyday Photos

Modern smartphone cameras do far more than capture pixels. Whenever you snap a photo, your device secretly embeds dozens of invisible metadata tags (known as EXIF & IPTC data) directly inside the image file:

📍 Exact GPS Location

Stores pinpoint latitude, longitude, and altitude—revealing your home address, workplace, or children's school location.

📱 Hardware Fingerprint

Logs your exact phone model, lens serial number, OS build, and unique camera sensor signatures.

🕒 Timestamp Audit Trail

Records exact date and time down to the second, making behavioral routine tracking trivial.

When posting images on classified marketplaces (OLX, Craigslist, Facebook Marketplace) or social forums (Reddit, Twitter), this hidden data is exposed to scrapers and bad actors.

How Photo Privacy Guard Works

Four specialized protection engines engineered to keep you anonymous and secure.

📍

1. Ghost Mode (Dummy GPS Injection)

Instead of simply deleting GPS data—which some social networks flag as suspicious or incomplete—Ghost Mode overwrites your genuine home coordinates with realistic tourist coordinates (e.g. Eiffel Tower Paris, Shibuya Crossing Tokyo, Times Square NYC).

• Converts decimal degrees into rational DMS IFD tags
• Preserves authentic camera structure while cloaking your location
🧹

2. Pure Privacy Wipe (Zero EXIF)

For users who want zero footprint, this engine extracts pure raw pixel data and rebuilds the image canvas from scratch, discarding 100% of EXIF, XMP, IPTC, ICC profiles, and thumbnail headers.

• Output: Pristine JPEG, PNG, or WebP
• Zero residual tracking bytes left in image header
🛡️

3. Anti-Fraud ID Card Watermarking

Identity theft through leaked CNIC, Passport, or utility bill photos is a severe risk in South Asia and globally. Fraudsters use leaked scans to register illegal SIM cards or take fraudulent loans.

• Overlays semi-transparent diagonal security text
• e.g. "FOR BANK ACCOUNT ONLY" or "SPECIMEN COPY"
• Keeps details readable for the recipient while voiding re-use
⬛

4. Smart Redaction & Censorship

Designed for classified ads and chat screenshots. Enables users to instantly mask or pixelate sensitive regions such as vehicle license plates, bank balances, account numbers, or faces.

• Gaussian blur & solid blackout bar modes
• Permanent pixel overwrite (cannot be reversed)
🤖

Seamless ChatGPT MCP Integration

Model Context Protocol 2024-11-05 Specification

Photo Privacy Guard is fully equipped as a standard Model Context Protocol server. AI agents in ChatGPT and Claude can call tools like inspect_photo_privacy, spoof_photo_location, and add_anti_fraud_watermark to sanitize media on the fly during natural conversations.

POST /mcp → JSON-RPC 2.0 Handshake (initialize, tools/list, tools/call) • Zero data logged.
